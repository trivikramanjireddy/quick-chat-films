# Streamline the CineQuick homepage

## What will change
- Remove the full “From Idea To Delivery” process section.
- Remove the full “We Create Visual Stories” about section.
- Replace “Numbers That Speak” with one concise “WHY CINEQUICK” section.
- Present these six proof points in a restrained cinematic layout:
  - Worked on 55+ Films
  - 100+ Commercials & Brands
  - Same-Day Delivery Available
  - On-Location Editing
  - Cinematic Quality Production
  - Dedicated Creative Team
- Reorder the homepage so real work, client proof, testimonials, and booking actions appear sooner.
- Reduce repeated or lower-priority content that creates unnecessary scrolling.

## Homepage flow
```text
Moving work gallery
→ Portfolio/work showcase
→ Trusted clients
→ Why CineQuick
→ Testimonials
→ Behind the scenes
→ Booking call to action
→ Supporting details and contact
```

## Visual direction
- Minimal black canvas with white typography and selective orange highlights.
- Large editorial heading, fine dividers, and six compact proof points instead of generic cards.
- Subtle staggered reveals only; no heavy decorative effects or template-like icon tiles.
- Two-column desktop composition and a compact mobile stack.

## Technical details
- Remove the obsolete section imports and renders from the homepage.
- Refactor the existing WhyChoose section with the new copy and responsive motion.
- Keep existing portfolio, testimonials, WhatsApp behavior, and other pages unchanged.
- Validate desktop and mobile layout, then confirm the preview builds cleanly.
